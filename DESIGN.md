# Design System: Acid-Anime / Neo-Chunky Studio Aesthetic

Inspired by the Aiko Hana reference design, tailored for Mhdi's software, AI, and creative product engineering portfolio.

## 1. Color Palette
- **Deep Obsidian (`bg-dark`)**: `#090B04` - Dominant dark background for high-contrast sections.
- **Olive Chartreuse (`bg-olive`)**: `#6e8000` / `#738600` - Signature full-bleed section background.
- **Electric Lime (`accent-lime`)**: `#ccff00` / `#d4ff00` - High-voltage attention grabber for bento cards, buttons, stars, and tags.
- **Card Dark (`surface-dark`)**: `#121509` / `#161a0b` - Rich dark surface with subtle lime-tinted borders.
- **Card Lime (`surface-lime`)**: `#ccff00` with dark text `#090b04` for high-impact bento cells.
- **Coral / Red Pop (`accent-coral`)**: `#ff3838` - Energetic pop for badges and graphic avatars.

## 2. Key Layout Components
1. **Untouched Video Hero**: The hero section with desktop/mobile background video is completely preserved as requested.
2. **"What I Build" (O Que Eu Faço)**:
   - Full-bleed Olive background (`#708200`) with watermark emblem.
   - 3 rounded vertical cards (`rounded-[28px]`) with colored header avatar capsules, crisp descriptions, and tech badges.
   - Bottom floating capsule with quantitative metrics (`+100 Solutions`, `+50K Users`, `+4 Core Systems`).
3. **"My Craft & Philosophy" (Meu Estilo)**:
   - Deep Obsidian background (`#090B04`).
   - Header with bold typography and electric lime 8-point sparkle badge.
   - Bento grid with asymmetric lime cards, diagonal arrows (`↙`, `↗`), pull quotes, and visual crops.
4. **"Start A Project / Contact" (Faça Seu Pedido)**:
   - Olive background with nested dark super-card.
   - High-impact neon lime CTA button, avatar illustration, and direct contact trigger.
